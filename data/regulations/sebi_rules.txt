SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) Regulations Guidelines

1. SEBI Block Deal Guidelines
A block deal is defined as a single transaction of a minimum quantity of 500,000 shares or a minimum value of ₹10,000,000 (1 Crore) executed on a separate trading window. It must be reported to the exchange immediately and executed during standard trading windows designated by the exchanges.

2. SEBI Exchange Trading Hours
Standard equity and derivative trading hours on Indian exchanges (NSE and BSE) are defined as 09:15 to 15:30 IST. For regulatory clearing and settlement audits, trading activity checks consider any order routing outside the extended clearing window of 09:00 to 17:00 IST as off-hours trading requiring authorization.

3. SEBI Algorithmic Trading Order-to-Trade Ratio (OTR)
To maintain market order, SEBI enforces strict guidelines regarding the Order-to-Trade Ratio (OTR) for algorithmic trading. Any high cancel rate where the daily volume of cancellations exceeds 10% of total placed orders triggers exchange algorithmic trading penalties and automated limits.

4. SEBI PFUTP Regulations (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices)
Under the SEBI PFUTP Regulations, any trade price or volume execution that deviates significantly (defined as more than 3 standard deviations from the historical asset mean) is flagged for front-running, wash trading, or price manipulation.
