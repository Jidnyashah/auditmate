RBI (Reserve Bank of India) Master Directions & KYC Guidelines

1. RBI Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Guidelines
Under the RBI Master Directions on KYC, all banking institutions and regulated entities must ensure mandatory compliance controls. Every financial transaction must include a verified customer_id (trader_id), transaction channel (venue), and counterparty name. Missing parameters constitute a critical KYC policy violation.

2. RBI Large Exposure Framework (LEF)
The RBI Large Exposure Framework is designed to manage credit risk and systemic exposure. The framework restricts a bank's exposure to a single counterparty or group of connected counterparties. A single counterparty exposure must not exceed 25% of the bank's eligible capital base or daily desk volume.

3. RBI Trade Reporting and CCIL Guidelines
All derivative and money market transactions must be reported to the designated Trade Repository (TR) operated by the Clearing Corporation of India (CCIL). Every transaction must contain a unique trade_id to ensure single-count submission. Duplicate Trade IDs are flagged as critical reporting failures.
