PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act) and FIU-IND Guidelines

1. Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) Framework
The PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act) governs AML and Terrorist Financing (TF) compliance inside India. Regulated entities must register all suspicious transactions with the Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND). High-risk transactions involve customers transacting with FATF-blacklisted or high-risk TF countries (such as Iran, North Korea, Syria, and Yemen).

2. AML / CFT Risk Profiling
Regulated financial entities are required to assign a customer risk profile (Low, Medium, High). High-risk customer activities must trigger enhanced diligence checks. Wires or transfers to high-risk jurisdictions require immediate compliance review.

3. Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) & Structuring
Under PMLA, "structuring" is the act of splitting a single large transaction into multiple smaller transactions to avoid triggering mandatory reporting limits (typically ₹1,000,000 / 10 Lakhs). Any series of transactions to high-risk destinations under the ₹10 Lakh threshold within a short time window must be flagged and reported to FIU-IND.
